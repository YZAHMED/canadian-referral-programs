# Canadian carrier referral programs — a verified, dated dataset

Eight Canadian mobile carriers' refer-a-friend programmes, each read from the
carrier's own website and published with the date it was checked.

**Last verified: 2026-10-21 · 8 carriers · 4 live, 4 discontinued**

Maintained alongside the human-readable version at
**<https://mobilereferrals.ca/carrier-referral-programs/>**

---

## Why this exists

Referral-code aggregators routinely publish programmes that no longer exist. At
the time of writing, several still advertise Virgin Plus and Fido referral
offers — **both ended in 2024–2025**. Nobody maintains a dated, sourced record,
so this is one.

The interesting finding is that **half of these programmes have ended**. Bell
closed both of its flanker programmes on the same day, and its own Bell-brand
programme ended that day too. Rogers closed both of its own, earlier and
separately. Of the carriers in this dataset, every surviving programme belongs to
TELUS or Quebecor.

The dataset does not cover every carrier. TELUS's own programme (a $50 bill
credit for each side) and Eastlink's (a $50 credit for the referrer) are also
live — checked on their own pages on 2026-10-21 — and are not in it yet.

## Method

1. Amounts are read from **the carrier's own** website or support documentation.
   Never from a coupon site or a referral aggregator.
2. Every record carries the `source` URL it was read from.
3. Where a carrier's own pages contradict each other, that is recorded rather
   than resolved. Fizz is the live example: its programme page says $25 in every
   region while its plan pages say $40 outside Quebec — for the new member and, as
   of October 2026, for the referrer too.
4. Anything that could not be confirmed is left blank. **A blank cell is
   publishable; a plausible guess is not.**

Every figure here was adversarially re-checked before publication — three of the
original records were rejected and re-verified rather than shipped.

## Known limits

- **Amounts change without notice.** Check the carrier's own page before acting.
- Public Mobile's referrer reward is 1 Public Point per 30 days. Its help pages
  now value a point at $1 ("Redeem points at a value of 1 point for $1"), and
  its rewards terms add that points "have no cash value".
- Freedom Mobile has **no code to type**. The referrer gets a unique referral
  link, the friend must claim the offer through it before activating, and the
  terms permit referring only "individuals with whom you have a personal
  relationship".

## Changes

- **2026-10-21** — All eight carriers re-checked. Fizz's plan-page FAQ now
  promises the referrer $40 outside Quebec as well (its programme page still
  says $25); Public Mobile now publishes a point's value ($1); Freedom gives the
  referrer a link rather than an invite to a named person. Corrected: "every
  major carrier" (TELUS and Eastlink run live programmes not yet in the
  dataset), "every surviving programme belongs to TELUS or Quebecor" (true only
  of the carriers listed here), and the claim that Public Mobile publishes no
  point value. The CSV is now generated by `build.py` alongside the page.
- **2026-09-08** — Fizz re-verified across 20 region-and-language checkpoints.
- **2026-09-01** — First published.
