Why this exists

Referral-code aggregators routinely publish programmes that no longer exist. At the time of writing, several still advertise Virgin Plus and Fido referral offers — both ended in 2024–2025. Nobody maintained a dated, sourced record, so this is one.

The finding worth noting is that half the industry has left. Bell closed both of its flanker programmes on the same day. Rogers closed both of its own, earlier and separately. Every surviving programme belongs to TELUS or Quebecor.

Method

  1. Amounts are read from the carrier's own website or support documentation. Never from a coupon site or a referral aggregator.
  2. Every record carries the source URL it was read from.
  3. Where a carrier's own pages contradict each other, that is recorded rather than resolved. Fizz is the live example: its programme page says $25 in every region while its plan pages advertise $40 outside Quebec.
  4. Anything that could not be confirmed is left blank. A blank cell is publishable; a plausible guess is not.

Figures are re-checked in a real browser rather than by fetching raw HTML, because several carriers ship every regional variant of a page and delete the non-matching ones in the browser — a check that reads the raw markup reports amounts no visitor can see.

Known limits

  • Amounts change without notice. Check the carrier's own page before acting.
  • Public Mobile's referrer reward is 1 Public Point per 30 days. The commonly quoted “1 point = $1” appears on no current Public Mobile page — its only source is a staff forum post from January 2022, so the dollar value is deliberately not asserted here.
  • Freedom Mobile has no shareable referral code. Its programme is invite-only and its terms permit referring only “individuals with whom you have a personal relationship”, so it cannot be used the way the others can.
